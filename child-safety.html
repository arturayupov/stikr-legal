Child safety standards

stikr. has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

  • Prohibited: any content that sexualizes minors, grooming, or exploitation. Such content is removed and accounts are blocked.
  • Prevention: photos and AI results pass automated moderation before generation and before anything becomes public; personal packs are private by default; public listing requires moderation.
  • Reporting: every public pack can be reported in the app; reports reach our team immediately. You can also report via @stikrme_bot.
  • Law enforcement: confirmed CSAM is reported to the relevant authorities (for example NCMEC in the United States) as required by law.
  • Age: the app is intended for users 13 and older.
  • Contact for child-safety matters: info@arcadallc.com or @stikrme_bot.
