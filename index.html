Common questions

Do I need an account? No. The app creates an anonymous account for your device. Nothing to sign up for.

Where are my stickers? Classic packs are stored only on your phone. AI packs are stored privately for your device account so you can export them.

My purchase is missing. Open Profile → Get credits → Restore purchases (subscriptions). For credit packs, contact us with the date and store receipt.

How do I delete my data? See Delete your data.
