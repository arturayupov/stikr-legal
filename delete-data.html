Delete your data

How to delete your stikr. account and data.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open stikr. → Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

This deletes your packs, stickers, credits, purchase history and usage records on our servers (usually within minutes), removes Classic packs from your phone, and starts a fresh anonymous account.

Without the app

Email info@arcadallc.com or message @stikrme_bot with the word delete and, if you can, the date of a purchase or a pack name, so we can find your device account. We confirm when it is done (within 30 days at most).

What may remain

  • Records stores keep about your purchases (Apple, Google, Stripe) — managed by them.
  • Stickers you already added to WhatsApp or Telegram stay there; delete them in those apps.
  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you.
