Privacy policy

Applies to the stikr. mobile apps and related services (“stikr.”, “we”), provided by Arcada LLC.

Short version

  • No sign-up: your account is an anonymous ID created for your device.
  • Classic mode works entirely on your phone — your photos never leave it.
  • AI mode uploads the photos you pick to our private servers only to make your stickers. They are deleted right after generation (and in any case within 24 hours) and are never used to train AI models.
  • Your packs are private unless you share them yourself.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use advertising trackers.

What we collect

  • Device account. A random account ID, app language, theme and region (to show trends for your country). No name, email or birth date.
  • Photos you choose for AI mode. Processed as described above. Classic mode photos are processed on your device only.
  • Stickers and packs. AI packs are stored privately for your account. Classic packs stay on your device unless you export them to Telegram (then the images are uploaded temporarily and deleted within 24 hours).
  • Purchases. Handled by Apple App Store, Google Play or Stripe through RevenueCat. We receive the product, transaction ID and status to add credits — never your card details.
  • Usage events. Anonymous product events (for example “pack created”, “exported to WhatsApp”) stored on our own servers, linked to the random account ID only. No advertising ID.
  • Crash reports. Technical error reports without personal data, on our self-hosted error tracker.
  • Talking stickers. The short phrase you type is turned into an AI voice; the sticker artwork is analysed once to find the mouth. We never record your own voice.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, a push token for your device (to tell you that a pack is ready or a daily drop is live).
  • Telegram export. If you add a pack to Telegram, our bot receives your Telegram user ID (Telegram requires it to create a sticker set for you).

How we use it

Only to provide the app: make and store your stickers, add them to WhatsApp or Telegram, process purchases and credits, keep the service safe (limits, moderation of public content, abuse prevention) and understand which features work.

Who processes data for us

We use these service providers (processors). Each receives only what is needed for the task below.

  • Google LLC — Gemini API (AI image generation): the photos and prompt of your AI request; the sticker artwork once per talking sticker (to find the mouth). Paid API — Google does not use this data to train its models.
  • OpenAI, L.L.C. — OpenAI API: fallback AI image generation (same data as above, only if Gemini is unavailable); automated moderation of photos, captions and phrases; the AI voice for talking stickers (the phrase you type). API data is not used for training.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany, EU): hosting of our servers — database, file storage, processing and our self-hosted error tracker (crash reports without personal data).
  • RevenueCat, Inc., Apple, Google Play and Stripe, Inc. (web purchases): payments and subscriptions. We never see card details.
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.): delivery of push notifications and of app updates.
  • Telegram and WhatsApp: only when you choose to export a pack; their own terms and privacy policies apply there.

Some providers are in the United States; transfers rely on their standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Retention

  • AI source photos: deleted after generation, at most 24 hours.
  • Packs and credits: until you delete them or delete your data.
  • Usage events and error reports: up to 12 months.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your data at any time in the app (Profile → Delete my data) or by asking us — see Delete your data. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK GDPR, Brazil LGPD, India DPDP Act) you may also have rights to access, correct or port your data and to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us at info@arcadallc.com or through @stikrme_bot.

Children

stikr. is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 uses the app, contact us and we will delete the data.
